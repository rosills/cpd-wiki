# B12D — B12 deficiency symptoms checker

A signs-and-symptoms checklist for suspected [B12 deficiency](../topics/vitamin-b12-deficiency.md), developed by Dr Chandy over many years of practice and also printed as Appendix 1 of his book *B12 Deficiency in Clinical Practice*. Read on 27 September 2026.

## Key points (as stated on the site)

- Available as a printable checklist and as an online app that gives an immediate read-out of B12 deficiency status; the online app was being upgraded and unavailable when read.
- Recommends following up with a blood test.
- States that **total serum B12 is a poor indicator** of deficiency, refers to a 200 ng/L threshold and to investigation of **active B12** testing, and reports the clinical observation that **symptoms may precede abnormal blood results**.

> **Open question:** The checklist's items and scoring weren't visible when read, and it cites no validation data. Look at the printable version, and compare with current NICE CKS and British Society for Haematology guidance on B12 deficiency.
