# LLM-maintained knowledge bases for learning and CPD

**What I did:** read Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern, the practitioner discussion around it and Google's Open Knowledge Format, prompted by a discussion in the AI in the NHS group; then set up a working system for my own learning.

**What I learnt:** see [topic page](../topics/llm-maintained-knowledge-bases.md). The key distinction is compile-once-and-maintain versus search-every-time. The key risk is that AI-written summaries can become confidently wrong and be mistaken for sources, so sources must stay separate from synthesis and important points need checking against the original.

**What I will do differently:** capture learning as I go (phone photos, voice notes, web clips) into a single inbox, with an explicit check for patient-identifiable content before anything is processed. As an appraiser, when an appraisee brings AI-assisted learning, ask the same questions as always: what did *they* learn and change?

**Reflection prompts** (for Richard to answer — not filled in by the agent):
- What would make me trust a page this system wrote about a clinical topic?
- Where is the line between AI-assisted reflection and AI-written reflection in an appraisal?
