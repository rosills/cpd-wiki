# Medical cannabis in UK practice

Cannabis-based medicinal products are governed in England by [NICE NG144](../guidelines/nice-ng144-cannabis-based-medicinal-products.md). Much current prescribing happens in private specialist clinics, some of which describe a focus on complex and multisystem presentations ([Hazlett Health](../sources/hazlett-health-website.md)).

## What primary care is likely to meet

- Patients already prescribed cannabis-based products privately, whose other medicines, driving and occupational questions land with the GP.
- Patients with complex multisystem conditions (EDS, MCAS, POTS), chronic pain or complex PTSD asking whether it could help them — the groups private clinics describe seeing.

> **Open question:** NG144's recommendations (who may initiate prescribing, and the position for chronic pain, nausea and vomiting, MS spasticity and epilepsy) have not yet been read and summarised here — the NICE site blocked automated access on 27 September 2026. To do from the guideline itself.
