Title: Fermented cabbage metabolites and intestinal barrier protection
Date: 27 October 2026

What I learnt:
I read and appraised Wei and Marco (Applied and Environmental Microbiology, 2025), a laboratory study testing whether sauerkraut protects the gut lining. Bacteria-free filtrates of fermented cabbage prevented inflammation-induced leakiness in a gut epithelial cell model, whereas raw cabbage and salt did not. Fermentation enriched lactate, phenyl-lactate, indole-3-lactate, GABA and many peptides and phenolics, but single compounds only partly reproduced the protection, suggesting the whole mixture matters. Shop-bought sauerkraut varied widely in live bacteria and contained tyramine. The evidence is in vitro only; human trials are few and small. [Richard to add or edit.]
