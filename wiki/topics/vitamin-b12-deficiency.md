# Vitamin B12 deficiency

A deficiency that can present through symptoms before blood tests become clearly abnormal, and where total serum B12 is an imperfect marker ([B12D symptoms checker](../sources/b12d-symptoms-checker.md)).

## Testing

- Total serum B12 is described by the B12D source as a poor indicator; a 200 ng/L threshold is referred to, and active B12 is under investigation as an alternative ([B12D](../sources/b12d-symptoms-checker.md)).
