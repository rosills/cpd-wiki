# Keith Grimes

Founder & CEO of Curistica. In the *AI in the NHS* group (26 September 2026) he described six months of building a personal knowledge base using the [Karpathy method](../sources/karpathy-llm-wiki-gist.md), run with open-source tooling and local models, with Obsidian as the reader. He also raised the costs and benefits of adopting Google's Open Knowledge Format. He has said he may write an article on his adapted method.
