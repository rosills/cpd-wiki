# Sauerkraut in humans — IBS pilot RCT and microbiota crossover trial

**What I did:** read and appraised two human trials of sauerkraut, following on from [the in vitro barrier study](2026-10-27-fermented-cabbage-gut-barrier.md).

**What I learnt:** *(draft — Richard to confirm)* in a 34-patient IBS pilot, symptom scores fell similarly with pasteurised and live sauerkraut, but there was no non-sauerkraut control. In 87 healthy adults, 100 g/day for 4 weeks didn't change microbiome diversity; a few species changed and only pasteurised sauerkraut raised serum SCFAs, though the two products differed in more than pasteurisation. Overall the effects don't seem to depend on live bacteria, and no human study has measured barrier function. See [topic](../topics/fermented-foods-and-gut-barrier.md).
