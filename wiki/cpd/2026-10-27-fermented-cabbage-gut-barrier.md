# Fermented cabbage metabolites and intestinal barrier protection

**What I did:** read and appraised Wei & Marco (2025), an open-access laboratory study of the sauerkraut metabolome and gut epithelial barrier function.

**What I learnt:** *(draft — Richard to confirm)* bacteria-free sauerkraut filtrates prevented inflammation-induced leakiness in Caco-2 monolayers, whereas raw cabbage did not; fermentation enriches lactate, phenyl-lactate, indole-3-lactate, GABA, peptides and phenolics; single compounds only partly reproduced the effect; commercial sauerkraut contained tyramine and varied widely in live bacteria. The evidence is in vitro only, with little human data. See [source](../sources/wei-marco-2025-fermented-cabbage-metabolome.md) and [topic](../topics/fermented-foods-and-gut-barrier.md).
