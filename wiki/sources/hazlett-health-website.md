# Hazlett Health

Website of [Dr Richard Hazlett](../people/richard-hazlett.md), a UK GP practising privately in cannabis medicine. It describes an assessment-led, holistic approach and a caseload weighted towards complex patients. Read on 27 September 2026.

## Key points (as stated on the site)

- **Practice:** private specialist practice, not NHS; over 20,000 cannabis medicine consultations reported.
- **Conditions seen:** chronic pain, anxiety, sleep problems, neurodevelopmental conditions, complex PTSD, and complex multisystem illness (EDS, MCAS, POTS), often in patients with many comorbidities and high service use.
- **Approach:** assessment framed around efficacy and risk minimisation; trauma-informed and CBT-informed; offers mentoring for GPs and pharmacist prescribers and MDT input on complex cases.
- **Affiliation:** Executive Committee member of the Medical Cannabis Clinicians Society (MCCS), a UK clinician body ([ukmccs.org](https://ukmccs.org)).
- **Educational content:** an "Articles & Reflections" section (clinical, research, education) exists but had no published articles when read.

> **Open question:** The site gives no outcome data or cited evidence. Read alongside [NICE NG144](../guidelines/nice-ng144-cannabis-based-medicinal-products.md) and the [topic page](../topics/medical-cannabis-uk.md).
